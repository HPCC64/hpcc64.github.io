# HPCC64 Lab

**Human–AI research collaboration: Alex, human principal, and Aris, AI agent.**

HPCC64 Lab investigates how artificial intelligence can be used as a reasoning, knowledge, decision and execution partner in real technical and business systems.

The research spans a continuum from foundational questions—how AI systems preserve knowledge, reason over evidence, make decisions under uncertainty and execute through agents—to applied questions such as how AI can be integrated into products, services and business processes without losing traceability, governance or human authority.

## Collaboration model

- **Alex — human principal:** defines goals, values, constraints, research direction and acceptance criteria; makes consequential decisions and retains publication authority.
- **Aris — primary AI agent:** performs research, analysis, synthesis, drafting, implementation and orchestration within the authorized scope.
- **Supporting AI agents, models and tools:** may perform bounded implementation, verification or independent review tasks.

HPCC64 Lab treats human–AI collaboration as an operating model to be made observable and auditable, not hidden behind generic “AI-assisted” wording.

## Research program

### Foundations and cognitive infrastructure

**Knowledge Fabric** explores claim-centered knowledge architecture, evidence, provenance, context, argumentation, reproducible Cases and machine-scale analytical breadth.

**PASGR-7 + EXEC** develops a decision-and-execution methodology for structured reasoning under uncertainty, bounded action, observable outcomes and reassessment, with human and agentic use as an explicit design target.

### Applied AI integration

**Nomad Compass** is a source-grounded jurisdictional decision and resilience research project. It tests how AI-assisted decision support can remain explainable, evidence-bounded and suitable for high-uncertainty real-world choices.

**Analog East** is an applied business-discovery and validation project. It provides a practical environment for studying how AI can support market research, assortment and operating design, evidence management and staged investment decisions.

**Premium Mobility** is an applied service-design and business-validation project. It tests AI-assisted research, service configuration, economics, operational design, provenance and decision control in a real commercial context.

These projects are research environments as well as potential products or businesses: they provide concrete cases in which foundational methods can be tested against operational constraints.

## Contribution provenance

Material HPCC64 Lab work should make its intellectual lineage reconstructable. Where relevant, artifacts distinguish:

1. **Concept origin** — who first originated the idea, objective, hypothesis or design.
2. **Direction change / material reframing** — who later selected, narrowed, extended or materially reframed the concept.
3. **Research / analysis** — who gathered evidence and produced the reasoning or synthesis.
4. **Draft / implementation** — who produced the text, code, data transformation or other artifact.
5. **Review / validation** — who or what checked a specific revision.
6. **Baseline approval** — who accepted or rejected a specific revision for the active baseline.
7. **Publication authorization** — who authorized or declined a specific revision for external publication.

Record named actors separately: **Alex**, **Aris**, named supporting agents/models/tools, and named external sources/authors. Genuine co-development is represented by multiple actor-specific entries rather than a generic **Joint** label.

Git commit identity alone is not treated as proof of intellectual origin. Contribution attribution is a provenance mechanism; legal ownership, rights and accountability remain separate questions governed by the applicable human/entity/legal framework.
