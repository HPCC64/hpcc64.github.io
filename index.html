HPCC64 Lab

Human–AI research collaboration

Alex, human principal, and Aris, AI agent, research how AI can preserve and transform knowledge, reason under uncertainty, execute through agents, and integrate into real technical and business systems.

How the Lab works

Alex — human principal

Defines goals, values, constraints and research direction; makes consequential decisions and retains acceptance and publication authority.

Aris — primary AI agent

Performs research, analysis, synthesis, drafting, implementation and orchestration within the authorized scope, while preserving evidence, uncertainty and provenance.

Traceable contribution, not opaque assistance

Material work distinguishes concept and direction, research and analysis, drafting or implementation, review and validation, baseline approval, and publication authorization. This makes human and machine contributions reconstructable rather than hiding them behind a generic “AI-assisted” label.

Git commit identity is not treated as proof of intellectual origin. Contribution provenance is recorded separately from legal ownership, rights and accountability.

Technology due diligence, IT compliance, mission-critical systems, business continuity, data governance and lifecycle management, AI governance, hybrid multi-cloud, confidential computing, open technologies, interoperability and digital sovereignty.
