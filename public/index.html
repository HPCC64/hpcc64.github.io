HPCC64 Lab

Researching knowledge, decisions and human–AI collaboration.

HPCC64 Lab investigates how people and AI can use incomplete evidence to make reasoned decisions and carry out controlled action in technological, business and social systems.

The aim is not to claim certainty, but to make assumptions, authority, trade-offs and results open to examination.

01 / Operating model

Distinct roles, accountable decisions.

Human principal

Alex sets purpose, priorities, constraints and acceptable risk, and retains consequential decisions and final accountability.

AI collaborator

AI assists with research, analysis, design, implementation and verification within delegated limits; unresolved questions are escalated.

02 / Research questions

Study what systems actually do.

  • Resilient technology and operations

    What dependencies and failure modes matter, and what evidence shows that a system can recover under degraded conditions?

  • Knowledge and human–AI work

    How can claims, provenance and competing interpretations remain traceable when models, tools or operating conditions change?

  • Business and social systems

    How do incentives, institutions and feedback affect outcomes, especially where formal descriptions differ from practice?

Open standards, portable knowledge and replaceable components are considered where they preserve practical choices and resilience.

03 / Developing methods

Connect evidence, decisions and action.

The approaches below are under development. Their usefulness and limits must be tested in specific applications; the descriptions are not claims of universal validation.

  • Risk-Guided Value Engineering (RGVE)

    A cross-domain approach to comparing value, costs and uncertainty. MTQR means Money, Time, Quality and Risk / Uncertainty; Risk-Guided Technology Advisory (RGTA) applies this perspective to IT.

  • Required state and current state

    IT compliance assessment identifies obligations and constraints; technology due diligence examines the actual architecture and operating capability. Their comparison exposes gaps and alternatives.

  • PASGR-7 and EXEC

    PASGR-7 structures a decision through alternatives, scenarios and trade-offs. EXEC develops a bounded way to act, check results, handle errors and reassess.

  • Knowledge Fabric

    A developing architecture for traceable claims, sources, context, uncertainty and competing interpretations, intended to make decision knowledge reusable across tools.

Why “64”? The name denotes a frame of six system domains considered through four decision criteria — not a claim that six times four equals 64.

The domains are Value & Outcomes; Actors & Capabilities; Processes & Operating Model; Evidence, Data & Knowledge; Cognition, Decisions & Automation; and Technology, Infrastructure & Dependencies.
